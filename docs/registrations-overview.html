How Registrations work

An event goes from a draft in the office to a ticket on someone's phone, a scan at the door, and, if it has to be called off, refunds. Four parties take part: the organisers, the attendee, the app itself, and Reception at the door.

Organisers (office) Attendee (no sign-in) The app & emails Reception (door)

The whole flow

Solid arrows are the usual path. Dashed arrows are the branches: free tickets skip paying, cancelling can happen any time, and personal details are tidied away after the event. On a phone, scroll the diagram sideways.

Who does what

Organisers

Registrations → an event

  • Build the event: one or several dates, ticket types (free, paid, donation), limits, promo codes for all paid tickets or chosen ones.
  • Upload a picture and set its focus point, so the important part stays in view.
  • Choose in person, online or both. For in person, type the address and drop the map pin; directions go to the pin. For online, add the join link.
  • Publish to get the share link and a QR code. Close and reopen sales; a countdown shows when sales close.
  • Watch orders come in, open receipts over the page, mark payments, add notes, export to Excel.
  • Cancel the event and settle refunds. Delete only once every paid order is refunded.

Attendee

/register/… and /ticket/… (no account)

  • Opens the shared link, picks tickets and a date, adds names, applies a promo code.
  • Pays by bank transfer, the PayPal link, or at Reception, and uploads a receipt.
  • Gets an email with an Open ticket button. The ticket page shows the QR code, payment state and a countdown to the start.
  • For an online event, the join link appears once the ticket is confirmed and paid.
  • Can cancel their own ticket until they are checked in.
  • If the event is cancelled: bank and PayPal payers are refunded the way they paid; cash payers fill in a short bank details form.

Reception

/door

  • Points the camera at a ticket's QR code, or types the code.
  • A paid or free ticket for today checks in straight away, with no tap.
  • A ticket that still owes is held up with Check in and Paid now, check in.
  • Warns about cancelled tickets, tickets for another day, and people already checked in.
  • Registers walk-ins on the spot, paid or free.

The app

database rules + emails

  • Creates the order and its ticket code, and sends the confirmation email.
  • Puts the event on the church diary.
  • Notifies the organisers when a ticket type sells out.
  • Sends the "paid" email when payment is marked, and the "cancelled" email to everyone if the event is called off.
  • Blanks names, emails and phone numbers a set number of days after the event. Counts and totals stay.

Payments and refunds

Each order carries one payment state. It moves left to right, and organisers or Reception move it on.

A paid ticket Awaiting payment→ Receipt sent — being checked→ Paid or Paid (waived)
A free ticket, or 100% off with a promo code Nothing to pay Free with code REHOBOTH26
If the event is cancelled Refund owed→ Refunded

Bank details from the refund form can be seen only by people with the Refunds right. They are wiped as soon as the order is marked Refunded.

Who is allowed to do what

Each part of the work is its own right. Give a right to a role, or to one person, in Settings → Access & Privileges. The database checks every right, so hiding a button is never the only protection.

Safety built in

No back door

The register and ticket pages need no account, but they open nothing else. They can't sign anyone in or reach the app. A ticket page shows only that one order, found by its long random code.

Join link

An online event's link is never on the public page. It appears only on a ticket that is confirmed and paid, and goes away if the event is cancelled.

No lost money

A paid event can't be deleted while anyone is still owed a refund. Cancelling keeps every order and who paid.

Privacy

Personal details are blanked after the event. Receipts and refund details are visible only to people with the right to see them.
