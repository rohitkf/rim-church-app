# The app's short video

A twenty-second video about the app, in its dark theme and in two shapes, made with the `brag` skill
(`.claude/skills/brag/`, AGENTS §12) and rendered with Hyperframes.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `rim-app-landscape.mp4` | 1920×1080, for a screen, YouTube or a slide |
| `rim-app-landscape.jpg` | its poster (also the video's first frame, so it is the thumbnail) |
| `rim-app-vertical.mp4` | 1080×1920, for WhatsApp, Instagram and anything watched on a phone held upright |
| `rim-app-vertical.jpg` | its poster |

**A caption to post with it**

> Sunday doesn't run itself. This is the app our church runs it on: who is serving, when they
> are in, and what has to be ready before the doors open.
>
> Music: Sascha Ende, ende.app (CC BY 4.0)

**The music credit is not optional.** The track is "Happy Beats / Business Moves Vol. 10" by
Sascha Ende (ende.app), licensed CC BY 4.0: wherever the video is posted, the line above goes
with it. The sound effects are Kenney's (CC0).

**What is on screen.** Five screens of the built app at phone size, run on test data. The only
name shown, "Grace Mensah", is made up; nothing from the church's own records appears. The blue
"RIM" tile is the mark the app's header carries, not the uploaded App Logo.

The story, in order: "Sunday doesn't run itself" · who is serving (Can you serve? → Give
availability → the Availability Tracker) · when they are in (the Team Rota's 07:00 call time) ·
what has to be ready (Checklists and the Service Planner; Done → Head → Sign-off) · "Sunday, put
together."
